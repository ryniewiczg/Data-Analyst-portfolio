Pharma Asset & Spend Analysis

Client: Pharmaceutical Industry
Markets: Belgium, United Kingdom, United States
Tool: Microsoft Power BI

#Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed for a pharmaceutical client to:

- monitor assets across multiple countries,

- analyze contract vs ad-hoc spending,

- support decision-making related to asset maintenance, management, and contracting strategies.

The dashboard is built to enable quick cross-country comparisons (Belgium, UK, US) and trend analysis over time.

#Dashboard Key Features

KPI Overview
- Assets on Site
- Active vs Inactive Assets
- Assets on Multi-Year Purchase Orders

Total Spend Over Time
- Contract spend vs Ad-hoc spend
- Year-over-year trend analysis

Triage Summary
- Asset classification (Maintain, Manage, Contract)

Identification of categories with the highest asset volumes
- Contract vs Ad-hoc Spend Breakdown
- Cost structure visibility and control

Top Vendors
- Vendor ranking based on total spend

Maintain Parts Spend
- Analysis of maintenance and parts costs

Interactive Filters
- Year
- Country / Market

#Key Business Insights

Contract spend represents the largest share of total costs across all markets.
Significant differences in asset triage structure are visible between Belgium, the UK, and the US.
A small number of key vendors account for a large proportion of total spend, indicating opportunities for:
- contract renegotiation, vendor consolidation.

Increasing levels of inactive assets may drive unnecessary maintenance and operational costs.

The dashboard highlights years with higher cost exposure, supporting proactive budget planning.
