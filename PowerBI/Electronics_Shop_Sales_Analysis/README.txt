Electronics Shop -- Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)Project OverviewThis project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzingsales performance of an electronics retailer across regions, customersegments, and warranty types.The report delivers business insights into revenue trends, customergrowth, transaction behavior, and product warranty impact, supportingdata-driven decision-making at both operational and strategic levels.Business ProblemThe electronics retailer needed:- A centralized view of total sales performance\- Regional comparison of sales and transactions\- Rolling 30-day performance monitoring\- Customer growth tracking\- Analysis of warranty type impact on sales\- VIP customer sales monitoringThe goal was to transform transactional sales data into actionable KPIsand executive-level insights.Dashboard Highlights:KPI Overview:Total Customers (to date)Average Transaction Value (Last 30 Days)Sales Last 30 DaysTotal Sales Last Year (YoY comparison)Sales Trends (2-Year View)Monthly Total SalesMonthly Revenue TrendSeasonal and performance pattern analysisRegional PerformanceSales by Region (East, North, South, West)Number of Customers per RegionAverage Sales per RegionNumber of TransactionsWarranty AnalysisAverage Sales by Warranty Length (Short vs Long)Warranty-driven revenue impactVIP Sales TrackingMonthly VIP customer salesPerformance volatility trackingBenchmark comparisonData Model:The report is built using a Star Schema model.Fact TableFact_Sales - TransactionID\OrderDate\CustomerID\Region\ProductCategory\WarrantyType\SalesAmountDimension Tables (Logical Structure)Dim_Date\Dim_Customer\Dim_Region\Dim_Product\Dim_WarrantyRelationshipsFact_Sales[OrderDate] → Dim_Date[Date]\Fact_Sales[CustomerID] → Dim_Customer[CustomerID]\Fact_Sales[Region] → Dim_Region[Region]\Fact_Sales[ProductCategory] → Dim_Product[ProductCategory]\Fact_Sales[WarrantyType] → Dim_Warranty[WarrantyType]Filter direction: Single (Dimension → Fact)  Model type: Star SchemaKey DAX Measures:Total SalesTotal Sales = SUM(Fact_Sales[SalesAmount])Total TransactionsTotal Transactions = COUNT(Fact_Sales[TransactionID])Customers To DateCustomers To Date = DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Sales[CustomerID])Sales Last 30 DaysSales Last 30 Days =CALCULATE([Total Sales],DATESINPERIOD(Dim_Date[Date],MAX(Dim_Date[Date]),-30,DAY))Avg Sales Last 30 DaysAvg Sales Last 30 Days =CALCULATE(AVERAGE(Fact_Sales[SalesAmount]),DATESINPERIOD(Dim_Date[Date],MAX(Dim_Date[Date]),-30,DAY))Total Sales Last Year (YoY)Total Sales LY =CALCULATE([Total Sales],SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Date[Date]))Dataset:The original dataset is not included in this repository due to size andconfidentiality considerations.A synthetic sample dataset (`electronics_sales_sample.csv`) is providedfor demonstration purposes.The original dataset contained transactional sales data with: -
