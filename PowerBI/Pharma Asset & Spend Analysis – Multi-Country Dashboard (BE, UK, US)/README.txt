Pharma Asset & Spend Analysis

A Power BI dashboard analyzing asset management and operational spending for a pharmaceutical business scenario across three markets: Belgium, the United Kingdom, and the United States.
The report provides insights into asset utilization, contract versus ad-hoc spending, vendor costs, and operational efficiency to support strategic asset management and budgeting decisions


Project Overview

The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders monitor asset activity and spending patterns across multiple countries. It enables cross-market comparisons and identifies areas where cost optimization and vendor management improvements may be required.

The analysis focuses on:
- Asset monitoring across markets
- Contract vs ad-hoc spending analysis
- Vendor cost concentration
- Asset triage classification (Maintain, Manage, Contract)


Dashboard Features
KPI Overview:
- Assets currently on site
- Active vs inactive assets
- Assets on multi-year purchase orders

Spend Analysis:
- Total spend trends over time
- Contract vs ad-hoc spending comparison
- Year-over-year cost analysis

Asset Triage:
- Asset classification (Maintain, Manage, Contract)
- Identification of categories with the highest asset volumes

Vendor Analysis:
- Top vendors ranked by total spend
- Vendor cost concentration analysis

Maintenance Cost Monitoring:
- Analysis of maintenance and parts spending

Interactive Filters:
- Year
- Country / market


Key Business Insights
- Contract spend represents the largest share of total costs across all markets.
- Significant differences in asset triage structure exist between Belgium, the UK, and the US.
- A small number of vendors account for a large share of total spending, indicating potential opportunities for vendor consolidation or contract renegotiation.
- Increasing levels of inactive assets may lead to unnecessary maintenance and operational costs.
- The dashboard highlights periods with higher cost exposure, supporting proactive budget planning.
