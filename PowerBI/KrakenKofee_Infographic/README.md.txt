# Kraken Koffee – 2023 Half-Time Sales Review Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance for Kraken Koffee’s new Florida region, covering three store locations: Miami, Orlando, and Tampa.
The report explores product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and revenue trends while providing a sales projection for the full year based on the 2023 trajectory.

Project Overview
The dashboard was designed to provide a clear overview of operational performance across the newly opened Florida stores. It highlights key KPIs, identifies top-performing products, and analyzes customer purchasing behavior across different times of the day and week.

The report focuses on three main analytical areas:
1. Store Performance – revenue and transaction analysis across locations
2. Customer Behavior – purchasing patterns by hour and day
3. Sales Forecasting – projected year-end revenue based on current trends

- Location-based performance comparison

- Clean infographic-style dashboard layout 


Key Business Insights

Overall Sales Performance
- Mid-2023 sales reached $698K across 149K transactions.
- Average Order Value (AOV): $4.69.
- Best-selling item by quantity: Crow’s Nest Croissant.
- Highest revenue product: Davy Jones’ Sustainably Grown Organic Hot Chocolate (Lg).

Product Drivers
Category contribution:
- Coffee – 38.6%
- Tea – 28.1%
- Bakery – 11.8%
- Most top-performing items belong to coffee and tea categories.

Customer Behavior Patterns
- Peak purchasing time occurs around 10:00 AM.
- Monday generates the highest revenue, while Saturday has the lowest.
- Transactions before 7 AM and after 7 PM represent only 3.6% of total revenue.
- These patterns suggest potential optimization of store operating hours.

2023 Sales Forecast
- Projected year-end revenue: $1.409M
- Stretch target: $1.59M
- Strong growth between April and June indicates the target may be achievable.
