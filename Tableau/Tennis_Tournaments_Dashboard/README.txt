Tennis Performance Dashboard (Tableau)

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing professional tennis players’ performance across tournaments, surfaces, and career earnings.

The project focuses on comparing player achievements, identifying performance patterns across different surfaces, and exploring relationships between success metrics such as titles, finals, and prize money.

Project Overview

Professional tennis performance can be evaluated through multiple dimensions, including tournament wins, surface specialization, and career earnings. Understanding these factors helps uncover player strengths and historical trends in the sport.

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of player performance across Grand Slams, surfaces, and overall career statistics.

The analysis focuses on:

distribution of wins across different surfaces

comparison of Grand Slam performance

relationship between titles and career prize money

finals performance (wins vs losses)

Dashboard Features

Surface performance overview (Clay, Grass, Hard, Carpet)

Career prize money vs total titles scatter plot

Grand Slam wins breakdown by tournament

Grand Slam distribution by player (percentage view)

Finals win percentage comparison

Finals played vs finals lost analysis

Total tournament wins by player

Geographic distribution of player origins

Interactive filters (player, tournament selection)

Key Insights

Hard courts dominate as the most successful surface across players.

Top players (Djokovic, Nadal, Federer) clearly stand out in both titles and earnings.

Rafael Nadal shows strong specialization on clay courts.

Some players maintain high finals win percentages despite fewer appearances.

Grand Slam distribution highlights clear tournament preferences and dominance patterns.

European players dominate the top rankings in both titles and prize money.

Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared in Excel before visualization in Tableau.

Preparation steps included:

cleaning and standardizing player names and categories

aggregating wins by surface and tournament type

calculating finals win percentages

structuring data for geographic visualization

creating calculated fields for comparative analysis
