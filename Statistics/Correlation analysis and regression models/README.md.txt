Employee Data Analysis – Statistical Insights (Excel)

An Excel-based statistical analysis of employee data exploring salary distribution, work experience, job satisfaction, and departmental patterns.

The project applies descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, and simple regression to understand relationships between key HR metrics.


Project Overview

This analysis examines employee data to identify patterns in compensation, experience, workload, and satisfaction levels.

The project focuses on:
- descriptive statistics for key HR metrics  
- correlation analysis between experience, salary, and satisfaction  
- regression analysis exploring predictors of job satisfaction  
- comparison of salary dispersion across departments  


Dataset
The dataset contains records for 100 employees and includes the following fields:
- Salary  
- Experience_Years  
- Education_Years  
- Satisfaction_Score  
- Hours_Worked_Per_Week  
- Annual_Leave_Days  
- Department  


Analysis Areas:

Descriptive Statistics
Measures of central tendency and dispersion were calculated to understand the distribution of employee characteristics, including:
- mean, median, and mode  
- variance and standard deviation  
- range and distribution patterns  

Correlation Analysis
Correlation analysis was used to evaluate relationships between key variables, including:

- experience vs salary  
- working hours vs job satisfaction  

Regression Analysis
A simple linear regression model was applied to evaluate whether **experience predicts job satisfaction**.

Model:
Satisfaction = β₀ + β₁ × Experience


Visualizations
The analysis includes visual representations such as:

- Histogram of salary distribution  
- Scatter plot illustrating relationships between key variables  


Key Insights
- Salary distribution is concentrated within a mid-range band.  
- Experience shows **very weak correlation with salary and satisfaction**.  
- The regression model explains **almost none of the variation in job satisfaction (R² ≈ 0%)**.  
- Salary dispersion differs significantly across departments.  
- The **IT department shows much greater salary variability than HR**.
